DailyBudget

Daily income & expenses • Budgets • Bills • Goals

—

Take control of your money—one day at a time.

DailyBudget helps you record income and expenses, plan daily limits, track monthly category budgets, manage recurring bills, and visualize progress toward savings goals. Built for non-technical users, with real authentication and database-enforced privacy via Supabase RLS.

Dashboard that makes sense Balance, daily spend, monthly progress, upcoming bills.
Easy data entry Add/edit/delete transactions with validation and search.
Budget planner Daily and monthly category limits + alerts.
Reports & CSV export Charts and safe CSV export for your data.
Automobile maintenance & control: This app includes a car maintenance tracker stored locally in your browser (not in Supabase). It’s meant as a non-sensitive convenience feature.
